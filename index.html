About the Lab

The Structural Parasitology and Protein Engineering Laboratory at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, led by Dr. Soumyananda Chakraborti, explores molecular mechanisms governing parasite survival and host interaction.

Our research focuses on iron metabolism and therapeutic targeting.

We also develop protein nanocage systems for drug delivery and antigen display.

Research Areas

Structural Parasitology

Mechanisms of parasite survival and virulence.

Iron Metabolism

Targeting essential biological pathways.

Protein Engineering

Nanocage-based delivery systems.
