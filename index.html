About Us

We investigate molecular mechanisms of parasitic survival using cutting-edge structural biology techniques, cryo-EM, and X-ray crystallography. Our protein engineering platform develops next-generation therapeutics targeting iron metabolism pathways and novel protein nanocages for drug delivery.

Research Areas

Iron Metabolism

Structural analysis of parasite iron acquisition pathways and therapeutic targeting

Protein Engineering

Cryo-EM guided design of protein nanocages and engineered therapeutics

Drug Discovery

Structure-guided development of antiparasitic therapeutics
