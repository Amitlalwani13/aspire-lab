About the Lab

The Structural Parasitology and Protein Engineering Laboratory at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus led by Dr. Soumyananda Chakraborti.

We focus on cutting-edge research in parasite structural biology, iron metabolism pathways, and protein nanocage engineering for drug delivery.

Research Areas

Structural Parasitology

High-resolution structural analysis of parasite proteins and host-parasite interactions using cryo-EM and X-ray crystallography.

Iron Metabolism

Targeting iron acquisition pathways in parasites for novel therapeutic development.

Protein Engineering

Design of protein nanocages and engineered delivery systems for targeted therapeutics.
