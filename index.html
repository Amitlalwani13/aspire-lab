About the Lab

The Structural Parasitology and Protein Engineering Laboratory at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, led by Dr. Soumyananda Chakraborti, explores the molecular and structural foundations of key parasitic pathways that govern survival, virulence, and host interaction.

Our research emphasizes iron metabolism and other essential mechanisms that hold promise for therapeutic intervention.

Beyond infectious disease biology, the group pioneers protein nanocage engineering for targeted drug delivery and antigen display platforms.

By integrating structural biology, molecular parasitology, and translational biotechnology, we aim to transform discoveries into therapeutic strategies.

Research Areas

Structural Parasitology

Mechanisms of parasite survival and host interaction.

Iron Metabolism

Targeting essential pathways for drug development.

Protein Engineering

Nanocage systems for delivery and antigen display.
